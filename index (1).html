    • Recipe #1

    • Time: 1:00
    • Serves: 2 serves
    Ingredients Quantity
    milk 2 spoonfuls
    cheese 1/4 cup
    corn 1 cup
    sugar 2 tablespoonfull
    salt a pinch
    vanilla a dash

    Steps:

    1. Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat.
    2. Toss in the corn and sauté for 2–3 minutes until it softens and gets glossy.
    3. Pour in the milk and sugar, stir, and let it simmer gently for about 5 minutes.
    4. Add a pinch of salt to balance the sweetness.
    5. Mix in the cheese and stir until it melts into a smooth, creamy mix.
    6. Taste-test — if you want it sweeter, sprinkle a little more sugar.
    7. Serve warm in a bowl or chilled like pudding.

    Recipe#2

    • Time: 3:00
    • Serves: 4 serves
    Ingredients Quantity
    Bananas 2
    Eggs 2
    oats 1/4 cup
    baking powder 1/4 tablespoonfull
    salt a pinch

    Steps:

    1. Mash the bananas in a bowl till smooth.
    2. Crack in the eggs and whisk till combined.
    3. Add oats, baking powder, and salt. Stir till it’s like a pancake batter.
    4. Heat a pan, drop a lil oil or butter, and cook each side for 1–2 minutes till golden.
    5. Serve warm — fire with honey, syrup, or peanut butter.
